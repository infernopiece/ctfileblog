Today ~mid-day the lights went out, for the **longest blackout** in Ember's history! 

Written by Inferno (online alias)



Earlier, around 12-4pm people were walking around town, when suddenly without warning, the lights went out. People assumed it was just a small one, as per usual, but they were wrong. The blackout lasted more than seven minutes. 

> *"I was scared out of my mind! There was nothing I could do!"* 

 *\- Rein Lawson, local Street Sweeper* 

During the blackout, it was nearly complete silence, aside from the occasional shift. People had thought they were doomed. Many thought the lights would never come back on. One man had a panic attack, and was rushed to the hospital as soon as he was found, when the blackout ended. The mayor has addressed the issue and seems to say he can fix it!

> *"I felt like I couldn't breath... I was holding my breath, until I couldn't anymore! I was too scared to breathe."*

*\- Jane Juliet, local Desert Shop Owner*

There you have it folks! The **LONGEST** blackout in history happened today! We wish you luck, Ember News, out!
